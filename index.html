Who we take to India

Three categories. One provenance story India's premium consumer wants.

New Zealand is the cleanest, greenest premium provenance on the planet — pure water, clear air, certified sustainable production at the far edge of the world. For India's rising premium consumer, that purity is the luxury. No other origin among the fifteen-plus countries competing for the Indian shelf can tell this story.

Wine

Tariffs fall 66–83% over ten years

India's wine culture is at its infancy — which means the varietals, brands and price ladders are still being decided. We place boutique, family-owned wineries into the hotels, clubs and fine retail where those decisions get made, from Delhi NCR to Mumbai and Bengaluru.

Craft beer

A US$1b category taking shape

India's craft beer scene is growing fast and the FTA establishes a standing working group on alcoholic beverages. We build the importer relationships and premium on-trade presence now, so NZ brewers are the incumbents when liberalisation reaches beer.

Mānuka honey

First-ever Indian tariff concession

No other origin can say it: India cut mānuka tariffs 75% for New Zealand alone. For a wellness-obsessed, gifting-driven premium market, mānuka is the cleanest first-mover story in the whole agreement — and it needs to be claimed before the copycats arrive.

Why first movers win

In regulated markets, early distribution is the moat.

Indian alcohol retail runs on state licences, registered labels and established importer relationships. They take time to build and, once built, they defend themselves. The producers listed before tariffs fall will spend the next decade compounding; late arrivals will spend it negotiating.

  • Importer and state registrations secured pre-FTA
  • Listings in place as tariff cuts phase in from year one
  • Category benchmarks set by your brand, not your competitor's
  • MFN clause protects NZ's terms against later deals
