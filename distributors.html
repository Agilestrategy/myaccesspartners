Distributors: who moves our kind of product

Indian importers and distributors specialising in premium wine, spirits and gourmet food, ordered by size, independent and corporate separated, with the channels they sell into. Wealthy demographics only: 5-star hotels, clubs, fine retail, luxury gifting. Pick one and build their custom pitch.

Starting city: Chennai

Tamil Nadu is a state-monopoly market: every retail bottle passes through TASMAC, with premium imports sold through Elite outlets and licensed 5-star hotels and private clubs (Madras Club, Madras Race Club, Boat Club, ITC Grand Chola tier). The play: an importer partner registers our labels with TASMAC while MAP curates the premium NZ shelf and club listings. Mānuka honey needs no liquor licence, so it launches first through Chennai's gourmet retail (Amma Naana, Nilgiris premium) and e-commerce. Note TN's 2024-25 levy increases on imported liquor: price for prestige, win volume through clubs and hotels.
