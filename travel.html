The rule

No partner, no visit.

We only take guests to producers who are Market Access Partners: independent, family-owned, and signed with us for India. That is what makes the tour bespoke. Your guests taste at the cellar door in Central Otago and find the same label at their club in Chennai or Mumbai a month later. The story starts in New Zealand and keeps going at home.

  • Independent and family-owned producers only
  • Every stop hosted by the maker, not a tour desk
  • Wines, malts and honeys available in India through MAP
  • Small groups, private rooms, vegetarian menus arranged

The shape of a day

A winery in the morning. A distillery or an apiary in the afternoon.

Each day is three crafts and three experts, framed as provenance rather than consumption. A sommelier walks the vineyard and pours a private flight. After lunch, a distiller or a beekeeper explains why this water, this wood, this flower cannot be found anywhere else on earth.

Morning

Vineyard and sommelier

Private estate walk, terroir and varietals explained, a guided flight of four to six wines with the story of each vintage.

Afternoon

Whisky or mānuka

A single malt distillery tour and tasting, or a mānuka honey visit with live hives and a tasting across grades, MGO and UMF explained.

Evening

Long table

Dinner at the estate or a lakefront table, matched to the day's wines, with Indian palates and vegetarian guests catered for as standard.

The regions

Three modules. Combine them into the journey your guests want.

Module 01 · Arrival

Auckland and Waiheke Island

2 nights · Park Hyatt Auckland, Wynyard Quarter · ferry 40 min or helicopter 12 min to the island

Land, settle on the water at the Park Hyatt, and spend the first full day on Waiheke, an island of independent vineyards 40 minutes from the city. It is the softest possible landing in New Zealand and the first taste of the rule: island estates run by founding families, an island honey company, and the skyline on the sunset ferry home.

  • Day 1
    Check in, Park Hyatt AucklandHarbour welcome dinner at the hotel's signature restaurant. Early night after the long flight.
  • Day 2 am
    A beachfront Waiheke estatePrivate reserve tasting with the winemaker at a family-owned island vineyard; e-bike through the vines for the energetic.
  • Day 2 pm
    Second estate and long lunch, then island honeyA founding-family cellar door at Onetangi, then a guided honey tour in Oneroa with live hives and a tasting across grades.
  • Day 2 eve
    Sunset ferry to the cityOr helicopter back to the waterfront for guests who want the view.
Module 02 · North Island

Hawke's Bay

2 nights · one hour flight from Auckland · vineyard cottages or an Art Deco boutique hotel in Napier

New Zealand's oldest wine region and its warmest: Syrah, Bordeaux-style blends and Chardonnay from family estates under Te Mata Peak and on the Gimblett Gravels. The afternoons belong to a craft distillery in a historic Art Deco building and to the country's best-known mānuka honey visitor centre.

  • Day 3 am
    Fly to Napier, sommelier tasting at a founding-family estateThe estate walk, the flagship red, and the story of a family that has made wine here for two generations.
  • Day 3 pm
    Art Deco distilleryHandcrafted whisky, gin and rum in a heritage building on the Ahuriri waterfront. Tour, tasting, optional blending class.
  • Day 4 am
    Gimblett Gravels cellar tourA prestige tasting at a family-owned estate famous for single-vineyard wines and views to the ranges.
  • Day 4 pm
    Mānuka honey visitor centreObservation hives, a guided tasting across grades, and why mānuka grows here and nowhere else.
Module 03 · South Island

Central Otago and Queenstown

3 nights · lakefront five star in Queenstown · Bannockburn, Cromwell, Lake Hayes and Lake Wānaka

The New Zealand in every Indian honeymoon brochure is also the world's southernmost wine region. Pinot Noir grown on schist above turquoise lakes, a lakeside single malt distillery, a beekeeping suit-up with mānuka tasting, and a free Queenstown day for the adventures your guests came for.

  • Day 5
    Fly to Queenstown, beekeeping suit-up and mānuka tastingAn easy afternoon after the flight: hives, honey spinning and a graded tasting.
  • Day 6 am
    Winemaker-led Bannockburn tastingSmall groups only, at one of the most respected Pinot Noir producers in the world.
  • Day 6 pm
    Lakeside single malt distilleryNew Zealand's largest distillery on the shore at Cromwell: tour, tasting, and the view.
  • Day 7 am
    Tasting above Lake WānakaA biodynamic family estate rated one of the most beautiful vineyard settings anywhere.
  • Day 7 pm
    Lake Hayes cellar door and long lunchThen a free Queenstown day: Milford Sound, the gondola, or nothing at all.
