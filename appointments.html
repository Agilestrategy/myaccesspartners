How it works

  • The invitation is a real calendar invite (an .ics file) sent by email from your MAP address. Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail all show it with Accept and Decline buttons.
  • You are copied on every invite, so it appears in your own Google Calendar as well. Accept it there and it is on your calendar.
  • Cancel sends the guest a cancellation that removes it from their calendar. Resend sends the same invite again (useful if it went missing).
  • To change the time, cancel and send a new one.
  • Replies from the guest come back to your MAP address, so they land in your normal inbox.
