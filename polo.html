New Zealand polo ponies to India

Polo

Who we can supply: every Indian Polo Association centre, the Army, Navy and academy teams, the patron and corporate teams, and the New Zealand side that breeds and makes the horses. Ainsley Polo (Pukekohe) is the supply partner; the partner profile went to Kel and Lucy Ainsley on 14 September 2026 and nothing goes to Indian buyers until they have corrected it. Who to sell to first

The duty exemption decides the order. Sell first to the buyers who pay 5%, not 39.65%: the Army, Navy and CAPF teams, IPA affiliated civilian teams (five years of affiliation, 14 goal, up to 30 horses in two years) and rated players (men +1 and above, women minus 1 and above; six horses in three years). Each needs a certificate from the Department of Youth Affairs and Sports, and every horse needs a DGFT import authorisation. Live horses are excluded from the NZ India FTA, so there is no FTA relief. Export path: 30 days pre export isolation and an MPI certificate in NZ, then at least 21 days quarantine in India; NZ autumn shipment lands for the September season.

How a sale works

  1. Shiva opens the door with a patron or team, framed as a standing supply relationship, not a one off shipment.
  2. Buyer type decides the duty. Army, Navy, CAPF, affiliated civilian team or rated player: they apply for the Youth Affairs and Sports certificate; anyone else pays 39.65%.
  3. They come to see the horses. Host at the NZ Open, Clevedon, 21 February 2027 (the 50th), then Ainsley's at Pukekohe. The Maharaja of Jaipur was in Clevedon looking at ponies in January 2025.
  4. Price ex farm, MAP fee on top: 15% of the ex farm price on confirmation plus 5% from the Indian receiver on landing; exclusive route to India for three years. Indicative bands (assumptions, to confirm with the Ainsleys): green NZ$6k to 12k, club NZ$12k to 25k, proven medium goal NZ$25k to 50k, high goal NZ$50k plus.
  5. Export: DGFT import authorisation in India, 30 days pre export isolation and MPI certificate in NZ, flight via Singapore or the Gulf with an agent, 21 days Indian quarantine. Landed cost above ex farm price is estimated at NZ$20k to 35k a horse before duty (unverified). NZ autumn shipment for the September season.
  6. Volume: 10 ponies season one, then 15 to 20 a season, with the breeding programme and academy behind it from 2027.
