Assumptions & caveats: duty is applied to CIF value (FOB + commission where on-sold + freight/insurance). India's current wine tariff (~150%) is structured as 50% basic customs duty + 100% agriculture cess. FTA rates apply only from entry into force (expected H1 2027) and phase down over 10 years to 25% or 50% by value band. State excise, label registration and distribution fees differ by state and channel; the figure here is indicative. This is a planning tool, not a customs ruling — verify against the tariff schedule and a customs broker's calculation before quoting.
