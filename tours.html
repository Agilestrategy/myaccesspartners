Tour bookings: Club7 groups, partner stops, supplier notices

Every tour a travel partner books lands here. Build it from the itinerary modules, put a Market Access Partner producer on every stop, then send each producer their notice with one click. A booking can only be confirmed when every producer on it is a tour partner. No partner, no visit.
