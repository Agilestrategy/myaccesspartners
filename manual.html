The crew manual

One website, two faces. The public pages tell producers and Indian partners what we do. The crew pages behind the Google sign in run the business. The investor pages are the ones with rules attached, so they get the most space here.

1Getting in

  1. Open any crew page

    For example myaccesspartners.com/investors.html. You get a "Crew and partners" screen.

  2. Press "Continue with Google"

    Pick your @myaccesspartners.com account. Paul's founder account works too. Any other account is refused.

  3. You stay signed in

    Until you press Sign out (top right of any crew page) or clear your browser.

Partners and preview visitors

People who are not crew (a producer, Club7, Tusshar) get a link with the key on the end: myaccesspartners.com/?key=…. Ask Paul for it. It opens the public pages only.

Clients

Clients never sign in. Each family gets one private link to their own page. That is all they see.

2An investor, start to finish

Five steps. The Files page tells you which one you are on and what to do next.

  1. Open a file

    Files page, press + New file, type the name and email, press Open the file. Press Copy the invitation email and paste it into an email to the client. Done. The file now says "Filling in details".

  2. The client fills in their page

    Nothing for you to do. Their details and documents appear on the file as they go. If it goes quiet for a week, chase them.

  3. The client presses Submit

    You get an email at hello@myaccesspartners.com. The file turns green and says "Ready to send". Open it, glance at the documents, press Mark as sighted on the ones you have looked at.

  4. Send the package

    On the file, scroll to "3 · Send the package". Choose who from the list (or type any email address), check the ticked documents, press Send now. The email is written for you and goes from hello@myaccesspartners.com with the documents attached. It is recorded on the file.

  5. The adviser takes it on

    From here the licensed adviser talks to the client directly. We host the visit, keep the file tidy and never give an opinion on eligibility.

3The Files page

Investors, first tab. The list shows every family, what stage they are at and, in plain words, what to do next. Click a row to open the file.

The stages

New → Filling in details → Documents arriving → Ready to send → Package sent → With the adviser. The site moves the file along by itself as the client and you act. You can override it under "Notes, stage and who looks after it".

Documents

Click a file name to open it (the link works for 10 minutes). Mark as sighted means a crew member has looked at it; it shows as "(sighted)" in the package email. Reject asks you for a reason and keeps it out of packages.

Close file

Top right. The client's link stops working and the file drops out of the working list. Use it for families who walk away.

Notes

Facts and next actions only. Never write an opinion on whether a family qualifies; that is regulated advice and it is discoverable.

5Who we send to

Investors, second tab. This is the list that appears under "Send to" on every file.

  1. Pick the role

    Licensed immigration advisers, NZ law firms, Invest NZ approved funds, Indian counsel or accountants.

  2. Pick the firm or person

    Every licensed adviser, law firm and approved fund is already in the list with their published contact details. Tusshar Pawar and "To be appointed" are under Indian counsel.

  3. Check and press Add

    The form fills itself. Before the first introduction, confirm the adviser's licence on the IAA register or the lawyer's practising certificate on the Law Society register, and put the number in "Licence / reference".

To change someone, edit the row in the table and press Save. Untick Active to hide them from the Send to list without deleting anything.

6The fund matrix

Investors, third tab. Every fund on the Invest NZ list on one chart. Left is safer, right is riskier, higher is a bigger return the manager says it aims for.

  • Set three things: what the client wants (safe, balanced, growth), whether they need money back during the visa period, and any sector interest.
  • Read the three cards above the chart: the highest stated targets that fit. Then the table for the full shortlist.
  • Funds with no stated target are only in the table, not on the chart. Most venture funds are like this.
The ruleThe matrix builds a shortlist for the licensed adviser conversation. It is never shown to a client as a recommendation. Targets are the managers' own, unaudited and usually before fees. Check the live Invest NZ list on the day.

7The Rules page

Investors · Rules. Everything about how an Indian family gets New Zealand residence by investment, checked against the government sources on 17 September 2026. The sixty second version is at the top; the detail is in 13 numbered sections with a jump menu.

Show amounts in ₹

The button near the top converts every NZ$ figure on the page into rupees at today's rate, for orientation when talking to a family. Press again to switch back.

28 September 2026

Invest NZ reissues its guidance and fund list on this date. Section 13 has the check again routine. Nothing on the page goes to a client until it has been re-checked after that date, and monthly after.

8What we may and may not say

We may

Explain how the site works. Quote the published rules and amounts. Collect documents the client chooses to give us. Introduce licensed advisers, lawyers, funds and Indian counsel. Host the family's visit to New Zealand.

We may not

Say whether a family qualifies. Say which category or fund they should choose. Call a fund "government approved" (it is "on the Invest NZ list"). Say "golden passport". Fill in or lodge the application.

Why: immigration advice in New Zealand may only be given by an adviser licensed by the Immigration Advisers Authority or a New Zealand lawyer. Investment recommendations are regulated financial advice; Agile Strategy holds a FAP licence, Market Access Partners does not. Every email the site writes carries the introducer only wording for this reason.

10When something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"Crew only" after Google sign inYou picked the wrong Google account. Sign out and pick the @myaccesspartners.com one.
The client says their link does not workOpen their file. If it says Closed, the link is dead on purpose. Otherwise copy the link again and resend it; check they are not missing the end of it.
The client's documents are not showingPress "← All files" and open the file again to refresh it. Files over 25 MB or not PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC, Word or Excel are refused on the client's side.
"Send now" says email is not configuredThe Resend key in Supabase has been removed. Tell Paul. Use "Other ways to send" meanwhile.
The email went but the recipient did not get itAsk them to check spam for hello@myaccesspartners.com. The send is listed under "Sent so far" with its ID if Paul needs to look it up in Resend.
The rupee button says "placeholder rate"The live rate could not be fetched. The figure is approximate; refresh the page.
Anything elseTell Paul what page you were on and what you pressed. Nothing on this site can be broken by clicking around.
